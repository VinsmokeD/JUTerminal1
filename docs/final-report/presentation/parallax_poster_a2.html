Abstract// the problem

Cybersecurity students learn offensive execution and defensive visibility in separate courses — rarely seeing that an attacker's keystroke and a defender's alert are the same event.

Parallax closes that gap inside one browser. Students operate a Kali‑style terminal against deliberately vulnerable, Docker‑isolated targets while the matching SIEM telemetry streams live to a Blue Team analyst view. Every command, detection, note, score, and AI hint is linked into a single cause‑and‑effect timeline that ends in an automated debrief.
